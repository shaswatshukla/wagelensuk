# Paid monitoring backlog — plan only

No Phase 5 features are built. Estimates are engineering ranges, excluding legal review, procurement and security assessment.

| Priority | Feature | Effort | Main risks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Accounts, saved workers and monthly re-checks | 4–6 weeks | Personal data, retention, access control, breach response, stale rules |
| 2 | Reporting clock and opt-in reminders | 1–2 weeks | Correct trigger, holiday jurisdiction, notification delivery |
| 3 | Versioned SOC going-rate lookup | 2–3 weeks | Occupation mapping, discounts and transitional errors |
| 4 | Adviser-branded reports | 1–2 weeks | Misleading assurance, approved legal wording |
| 5 | Adviser multi-client workspace | 3–5 weeks | Tenant isolation, delegated permissions, exports |
| 6 | Right-to-work evidence tracker | 4–6 weeks | Sensitive documents, retention, expiry logic |

Any accounts or stored personal data require a fresh architecture and privacy review. Do not put personal data on an Always Free VM by extending this static deployment. The browser-only promise must be reconsidered explicitly before any monitoring product is introduced.
